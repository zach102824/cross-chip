{\large\bfseries Z-pair freeze in \code{exploring/}}
\hfill {\small Method~1 of \code{error_reduction_methods.md}; implemented in \code{freeze.py}}

\vspace{0.4em}
\hrule
\vspace{0.5em}

\paragraph{Setup.}
UCCSD doubles are compiled in the Jordan--Wigner (JW) encoding on an even register of
$N$ qubits with two spin rows: $\alpha$ on $0{\ldots}N/2{-}1$, $\beta$ on $N/2{\ldots}N{-}1$.
Each spin-paired double has a Pauli representative whose non-identity letters are
$Y$/$X$ on the excited orbitals plus a string of $Z$s (JW parity tails).
Those $Z$s always appear as \emph{spin pairs}: whenever $Z_q$ occurs with $q<N/2$,
so does $Z_{q+N/2}$.

\paragraph{Identity (exact on the reachable subspace).}
Starting from Hartree--Fock and applying only spin-paired doubles keeps $\alpha$ and
$\beta$ occupations locked: qubits $q$ and $q{+}N/2$ always have equal occupation.
Hence
\begin{equation*}
  \ZZ \;=\; (+1)\cdot(+1)\;\text{or}\;(-1)\cdot(-1)\;=\;+1
\end{equation*}
on every state the ansatz can reach.  Replacing each such pair by the scalar $+1$
is therefore exact---not an approximation.  In code this is deletion of both letters:
\begin{equation*}
  \underbrace{\ldots\,Z_q\,\ldots\,Z_{q+N/2}\,\ldots}_{\text{JW string}}
  \;\longrightarrow\;
  \underbrace{\ldots\,I\,\ldots\,I\,\ldots}_{\text{frozen string}}.
\end{equation*}
\code{freeze.freeze_z_pairs(string, keep_pairs)} performs this rewrite;
\code{fully_freeze} deletes every frezeable pair (\code{keep_pairs}$=\emptyset$).

\paragraph{Why freeze helps.}
Fewer non-identity Paulis $\Rightarrow$ shorter within-row CZ fan-in ladders when
compiling $\exp(-i\theta P/2)$.  Under the exploring error-budget proxy
(cross-chip 2q cost $\gg$ on-chip), deleting idle parity tails is a pure win.
Equivalence is checked by statevector overlap vs.\ the unfrozen circuit on the HF
bitstring (overlaps $\approx 1$ in \code{export_winners.py}).

\paragraph{Selective freeze: $Z$s as couriers.}
The freeze identity is bidirectional: any deleted $\ZZ$ may be \emph{re-inserted}
for free.  Retained pairs (indices $q\in$\code{keep_pairs}) act as parity
\emph{couriers} along the allowed within-spin CZ graph
(nearest-neighbour $+$ chords $(0,3)$ / $(N/2,N/2{+}3)$), so hubs and Steiner nodes
sit on legal edges.  \code{all_keep_masks} enumerates courier subsets; Method~F
(\code{method_disjoint_rzx_all_qubits}) searches masks $+$ disjoint $\alpha$--$\beta$
RZX hubs jointly.

\paragraph{Winners in \code{exploring/}.}
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{0.42\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Molecule & Freeze & Effect \\
\midrule
HF $6q$ & \emph{full}
&
\texttt{YZZXZZ}$\to$\texttt{YIIXII}, etc.\ $\to$ three weight-$2$ blocks;
RZX on $(0,3),(1,4),(2,5)$; \textbf{no CZ}. \\[0.35em]
Cl$_2$ $10q$ & \emph{selective}
&
\code{keep_pairs}$=\{\{2,3\},\{3\}\}$;
\texttt{IYZZXIXZZX}, \texttt{YIIZXXIIZX} stay weight $\ge 4$;
RZX on $(2,7),(0,5)$ with CZ fan-in. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

HF collapses to independent $Y\otimes X$ pairs because (after tapering) each double
has only one frezeable $\alpha$--$\beta$ support pair.  Untapered Cl$_2$ keeps shared
creation orbitals on qubits $4$ and $9$, so even maximal freeze leaves weight-$4$
strings---CZ ladders remain mandatory.  Couriers are kept only insofar as they enable
legal, vertex-disjoint hubs under the exploring connectivity rules.

\paragraph{Caveats.}
Exactness requires (i)~a spin-paired reference (HF), (ii)~only paired doubles in the
ansatz, (iii)~action on that locked subspace.  The measured Hamiltonian is unchanged;
only the ansatz Paulis are rewritten.  Re-verify overlaps if the doubles set changes.
